Interactive simulation

Read a scam text like an analyst

Scammers count on panic and speed. Toggle the switch to uncover the 3 deception hooks.

107700 900123
SMS Dispatch · Today 14:22
YourBank alert: your account has been locked. Confirm your details 2within 30 minutes or it will be closed. 3https://secure-yourbank-verify.com/login
You spotted 3 red flags 🎯
Attackers rely on artificial panic, spoofed numbers, and lookalike links. A real bank will never send a generic panic deadline or demand credentials over unsolicited SMS.
🚩 Red flag 1: Sender proves nothing
"07700 900123" is an unverified mobile number. Anyone can spoof sender headers or register disposable SIMs. A phone number never proves identity.
🚩 Red flag 2: The deadline is the hook
"Within 30 minutes" creates artificial urgency — a psychological trigger designed to force panic action before you can verify with your bank.
🚩 Red flag 3: The domain isn't your bank's and a padlock only means the connection is encrypted
secure-yourbank-verify.com is a fake lookalike. The browser padlock only means your connection to the scammer's server is encrypted, not that the site is real.
