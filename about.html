A plain-language cybersecurity publication that helps you understand today’s digital threats, real incidents and practical ways to stay safer online.

I started this blog because everywhere online people only talk about cybersecurity in technical terms, and most people are left with no real idea of how the digital world actually works. This is my attempt to fix that. I work in cybersecurity, but I write here for everyone.
